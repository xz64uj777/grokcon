# Ridge Ore – Round 2 update: River Valley

There's a second map now: **River Valley**, a wide river flat with a bridge, muddy banks, rain and silver in the river gravel. Your company, cash and machines are shared between the two maps. It's all one save.

Old saves still load. Nothing is taken away, and everything you own stays at Ridge pit until you haul it over.

## 1. Opening the valley

- Lease the **Deep bench** at Ridge pit (in the office, under Ground). That opens River Valley.
- Open the **Yard office**. The new travel row shows which map you're on.
- **Haul this machine** puts the machine you're in on a lowboy trailer and takes it to the other map for **$500**. The machine has to be empty. You can't haul the track crusher, or haul anything while a blast is going.
- Your first haul gives you the **River bars** lease for free. The **Valley quarry** (hard copper rock behind the valley camp) costs $30,000.
- **Go to River Valley / Go to Ridge pit** switches maps. It's free, but only works once you have a machine on the other side.
- **Crew** is a list per map. Once the valley is open, the office shows **Ridge pit** and **River Valley** rosters. Hire, repair, and work source stay on that map. A machine on the trailer stays on the lowboy's list.
- **Next** only cycles through the machines on the map you're looking at. The minimap shows the map you're on.

## 2. The bridge (30 tonne limit)

There's one bridge over the river. The sign says **30 T LIMIT**, and that counts the machine and its load together.

- Over the limit, the machine stops at the end of the bridge and a **TOO HEAVY** banner tells you how much it can carry. A dump truck can carry 18 t across. A heavy haul can carry 2 t.
- Backing off the bridge always works.
- **West barge:** a buyer tied up on the west bank. It pays about 70% of the rail price, and the load never has to cross. Sell there, or take a dump truck over the bridge for the full rail price.
- A hired dump truck still leaves room for the bridge and hauls to the rail. A hired heavy haul fills the bed and sells at the barge. It cannot take a real load across.

## The north road

Once the Deep bench is leased, a **lowboy** sits in the ridge yard. Get in it, pull up to an empty parked machine, and tap **Load**. The deck has three spots, and it holds two machines at most.

- Claim truck or skid steer: 1 spot. Two of them fit. A dozer and a skid steer fit.
- Bulldozer or payloader: 2 spots.
- Dump truck, excavator, or heavy haul: the whole deck, and it rides alone.
- The track crusher does not fit. Beds have to be empty.

Drive it north onto the gravel road and follow it to the valley camp. The road climbs through a mountain pass, with rock walls on both sides, a **PASS SUMMIT** pull-out at the halfway mark, and a **RIVER VALLEY** sign at the gate. The card counts the metres left while you are on it. Only the lowboy can take that road. Tap **Unload** wherever you stop; the machine rolls off behind the trailer onto open ground. The office button is still there: **$500**, one empty machine, no drive.

The pass counts as ridge country until it reaches the valley gate, so the office, shop and price board do not change halfway along an empty road.

## 3. Mud

The river banks are soft, so machines slow down there. Wheeled machines (pickup, skid steer, loader, trucks) slow down a lot. In deep mud they show **Bogged** and crawl. Tracked machines (dozer, excavator) barely notice. Nothing ever gets stuck for good, you just go slowly.

## 4. Rain

Some days a rain shower rolls through. It lasts one to two minutes and you'll see it coming down, with the sky darkening and fog closing in. Rain soaks the ground:

- The mud gets deeper and spreads, and low spots near the river flood into new mud.
- The ground dries slowly after the rain stops, taking about three minutes.
- The card shows the weather (Dry, Rain, Heavy rain or Wet ground). When you're in mud it also shows how much speed you're losing. Your first day is always dry.

## 5. Silver and the wash plant

- The river bars hold **silver** gravel, with a little gold gravel further south.
- The **wash plant** ($38,000, buy it in the office under Machines once the valley is open) sits on the east bank. It pays **$420 a tonne for silver** and **$300 a tonne for gold gravel**, before the daily price moves.
- The **rail loadout** at the valley camp takes ordinary ore at crusher prices, so valley copper doesn't need to cross the bridge.

## 6. The same as Round 1

- It's still phone only, with the same big thumb controls.
- It works on the Z Fold cover and inner screens and adjusts as you fold and unfold.
- Blasting is still a flat $750, once per face per day.

# Ridge Ore – Round 1 update: real controls and hands-on mining

This round adds realistic machine controls, hands-on mining and ore prices that change every day. It's all on the map you already know. The River Valley map comes in Round 2.

Old saves still load. Your cash, machines, land and piles all carry over. Faces you had already opened start with their dirt cleared off, so the update doesn't take anything from you.

---

## 1. Two ways to drive

Ridge Ore is a phone game, and everything is done with your thumbs. To switch between the two ways to drive, open the **Yard office** and tap **Controls**.

- **Realistic** (the new default): you work the arms, bucket, boom, blade and dump bed yourself. A clean, smooth scoop pays more.
- **Simple**: the old way. One stick drives, and you hold **DIG** while the machine does the work.

Even in Realistic you can hold **AUTO** to let the machine work by itself. It's just a bit sloppier than a careful hand.

## 2. Practice yard

Open the Yard office and tap **Practice yard**. You get a yard with one of every machine, all the land and 3 free blasts. Nothing you do there counts or saves. Tap **Leave practice** in the office to go back to your real company exactly as you left it.

---

## 3. Controls cheat sheet (phone)

On a digging machine the screen shows **two big sticks**, one for each thumb. A label under each stick tells you what it does on the machine you're in. The table below is generated from those labels. Do not edit it by hand.

| **Claim truck / Track crusher** | Drive | — |

- **Reversing:** pull the drive stick back. There is no gear button.
- **Excavator park:** tap **Park** to lock the tracks. The left stick then slews the house and crowds the dipper. Tap it again to drive.
- **Claim truck and track crusher:** one stick drives. In Simple mode, hold **DIG** to scoop. Realistic mode does not give them a bucket stick.
- **Skid steer and dozer:** push both sticks forward to go straight. Push one to turn. Push them opposite ways to spin in place.
- **Buttons above the sticks:** **Next** (switch machines), **Load** (load a truck), **Test hole**, **Blast** (only when you're at a face) and **Auto** (hold it to let the machine work).
- **Folding the phone** lets go of every stick, so the machine does not keep the last thumb input.
- **Top of the screen:** **Office**, **View** (change the camera), and sound on/off. Drag on the open ground to turn the camera, and pinch to zoom.

### The machine card

- On the folded front screen, the card with your machine's name, load and tips sits **at the top**, next to the mini-map, so it never covers the road ahead.
- **While you drive** it shrinks to one short line (machine, load, speed). When you stop it opens up again. **Tap it** any time to open or close it.
- The camera sits further back and higher on bigger machines, so you can always see where you're going.
- Ore face signs fit their names, and a sign that would be cut off at the edge of the screen hides until you turn toward it.

### Folding phones (like the Galaxy Z Fold)

- **Folded (front screen):** the sticks sit at the bottom corners with the buttons just above them. The machine card is at the top and warnings show just above the buttons.
- **Unfolded (big screen):** the sticks get a bit bigger and move into the lower corners where your thumbs rest. The buttons sit in a row between them, and the machine card sits at the top left, so it never covers your machine or the road.
- **Sideways:** same idea. Sticks go on the edges and the buttons go between them.
- **You can fold or unfold in the middle of a game.** The game doesn't reload. The controls rearrange themselves straight away, and every stick and button is let go, so your machine won't run away. A blast that's drilling keeps drilling as long as the machine stays still.
- Controls stay clear of the camera hole, the rounded corners and the gesture bar at the bottom.

### How to dig well

- **Scooping** (skid steer and payloader):
  1. Lower the bucket to the ground.
  2. Roll in **slowly**.
  3. Curl the bucket back.
  - Ramming in fast spills ore. Driving slowly fills the bucket clean.
- **Unloading**: raise the arms, then dump. If a truck is right in front of you, the load goes into the truck. At the crusher, reclaim or sluice, you get paid. Anywhere else it makes a pile on the ground.
- **Excavator**:
  1. Lower the boom, reach the arm out and curl the bucket slowly.
  2. Hold the swing still while you curl. Swinging or jerking the boom mid-scoop slops ore out.
  3. Swing until the bucket is over the truck, raise the boom and dump.
- **Bulldozer**: put the blade on the ground and push. The deeper you dig the blade in, the more it pushes, but the more it drags. Bury it all the way and the dozer **stalls**, so lift it a little.
- **Dump truck**: raise the bed at a selling spot to get paid. Drive fast with the bed up and the load slides off the back.

## 4. Hands-on mining

- **Dirt on top (overburden):** every ore face has a layer of dirt on top. Dig that off first.
  - In Simple mode, when you hold AUTO, or with your crew, the machine always strips the dirt first.
  - With your own hands: hold the bucket **halfway up** to scrape the top, or **all the way down** to dig the bottom.
  - Sell the dirt at the Reclaim spot (it pays for dirt) or dump it anywhere.
- **Purity:** every load of ore now has a **purity %**, shown next to your load. 90% pure gets 90% of the price.
  - Purity drops when your scoop is sloppy or fast.
  - It also drops when you dig the bottom while dirt is still sitting on top.
- **Test holes:** pull up to a face and tap **Test hole**, which costs $150. You'll see how clean the ore is, how much dirt is on top and whether it's hard rock.
- **Hard rock:** the Copper bench and the Deep bench faces are hard. They dig slowly until you blast them.
- **Benches (layers):** a pit goes down in steps. Every 100 tons you dig from a face takes it down one bench, and the next layer has fresh dirt on top to strip.
- **Wall slides:** if you keep digging the bottom while dirt is still on top, the wall gets steep.
  - The screen warns you: **"WALL STEEP."**
  - Keep going and the wall **slides**, dropping a pile of dirt back over the ore.
  - It's always avoidable: strip the top first and the wall stays safe.

## 5. Blasting

1. Pull up to a face and tap **Blast**. Each blast costs a flat **$750**, paid when drilling starts. The Blast button and the card show the price.
2. Hold still for **10 seconds** while the holes are drilled. If you drive off before it's done, the blast is called off and you get the $750 back.
3. A countdown starts. **Get every machine at least 22 m back.** The banner tells you how far away you are.
4. Boom: up to 70 tons of the face turns into a **loose pile** you can scoop right away, plus a pile of the dirt that was on top.
5. Machines left too close get **damaged** and run slower. Fix them in Yard office → Crew → **Repair**.

**One blast per face per day.** After a face has been blasted, its card says **"already blasted today"** until the next in-game day (5 minutes of play). You can still blast a different face.

## 6. Ore prices change every day

- One in-game day is 5 minutes of play. Every new day, each ore's price moves up or down at each selling spot, anywhere from **half to double** the normal price.
- See the board in Yard office → **Prices**. It shows today's price, a rising or falling arrow and the % of normal price.
- If you sell a big pile in one spot on one day, that price dips a little until the next day. You can't buy ore back, so the prices can't be gamed.
- Your crew automatically takes loads to whichever spot pays best today.

## 7. The pit looks real now

The same map, with the same faces in the same places, but it looks like a working pit:

- **Pit walls** step down in benches with rough, cracked rock faces and loose rock piled at the bottom, instead of flat boxes. You bump into them in the same places as before.
- **Ground** has dirt, gravel on the pit floor, and rock textures. The game draws these itself, so there's nothing big to download.
- **Haul roads** have tire tracks and low dirt berms along the edges. There are a few muddy **puddles**.
- **Boulders, scrub and trees** are scattered outside the pit.
- **Distant hills** fade into a warm, dusty haze under a soft sky.
- **Dust** kicks up behind every machine that's moving, not just yours.
- The sunlight is **warmer and softer**.

It's built to stay smooth on a phone. On a strong phone like the Z Fold the game uses sharper graphics, sharper shadows and more scenery. On weaker phones it automatically uses a lighter version.
